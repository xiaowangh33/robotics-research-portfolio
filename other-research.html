Physics-informed learning

Time-varying PINN for furnace heating

I extended a static-snapshot workflow to model a full heating cycle and nonuniform heat-source conditions. The implementation combines a 2D finite-difference reference path with PDE, initial-condition, boundary-condition, and reference losses.

Data-driven materials

Magnesium alloy property modeling

For a small materials dataset, I worked on data augmentation and regression workflows using a Transformer, MLP, and stacking approach to predict yield strength, elongation, and ultimate tensile strength from composition and process variables.
